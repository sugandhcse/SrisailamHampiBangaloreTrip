Trip Highlights

What made this journey unforgettable

🏛️

Lepakshi

Stunning 16th-century Vijayanagara temple with a hanging pillar that defies gravity and a giant Nandi bull.

🏔️

Gandikota

India's Grand Canyon — a stunning gorge carved by the Pennar River, best seen at golden hour.

🦇

Belum Caves

Second-longest natural cave system in the Indian subcontinent with dramatic underground formations.

🙏

Yaganti

A tranquil cave temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, surrounded by serene hills and cool springs.

⚡

Srisailam

Mallikarjuna Jyotirlinga — one of the 12 sacred shrines of Lord Shiva. Set by the Krishna river.

🌅

Hampi

UNESCO World Heritage Site. Ancient ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire amid dramatic boulder landscapes.

🚗

Mahindra XUV 300

Our trusty black steed for 1,450 km of highways, forest roads and ghats — not a single breakdown!
